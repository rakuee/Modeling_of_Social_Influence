# Modeling_of_Social_Influence

## Question to answer

How does what a person thinks change according to their environment?

## General idea

This project models opinion dynamics and social influence. Each person is represented as an **agent** with an opinion and an environment made up of family, friends, neighbors, work, media, and social networks. Agents interact with one another and update their opinions based on the influence of their surroundings.

The environment can influence opinion through exposure, conformity, persuasion, identity, and algorithms. The goal is to explore how individual opinions change and how collective patterns such as consensus, polarization, or echo chambers can emerge.
